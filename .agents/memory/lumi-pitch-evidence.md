---
name: Lumi pitch evidence boundaries
description: Founder-reported business signals and rules for future pitch and commercial claims.
---

Treat the original pitch's waitlist, Instagram reach, specialist interviews and school trial interest as founder-reported early interest, not independently verified traction, revenue or contracts.

**Why:** The source was a founder-supplied presentation, not underlying analytics, paid invoices or agreements.

**How to apply:** Preserve the evidence label in future decks until primary proof is supplied. The proposed school and family prices are hypotheses; do not imply validated willingness to pay or established unit economics.

Do not sum dyslexia, autism and broad learning-difficulty prevalence to size the market, or label all school-age children as reachable customers.

**Why:** Conditions overlap, enrollment is not an exact age cohort, and adult consent, product fit, access and purchasing constraints materially narrow the market.

**How to apply:** Distinguish a prevalence-based opportunity scenario from verified SAM and bottom-up paid-seat targets. Do not carry forward the original unsupported $78M market or 2029 revenue projection as facts.
